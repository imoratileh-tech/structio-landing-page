Frequently asked questions.

What is Structio?

Structio is a system for managing inbound property enquiries — from the first response through qualification, CRM capture and follow-up — built for South African real-estate professionals.

Does Structio replace my CRM?

No. Structio feeds structured, qualified information into the CRM you already use, rather than replacing it.

What parts of the enquiry journey can Structio automate?

The repetitive operational parts: initial response, qualification, CRM data capture and follow-up. Relationships, viewings, valuations and negotiation stay with your team.

Can the workflow be customised?

Yes. Each workflow is configured around how your business actually handles enquiries, not a fixed template.

What happens when a human needs to get involved?

The workflow is designed to hand off cleanly — surfacing the right information and the right next action so your team can step in at the right point.

Can Structio work with existing systems?

Structio is built to connect to supported systems as part of the implementation process. We'll confirm compatibility with your specific tools during the mapping stage, rather than assuming it upfront.

How is information handled?

Only information relevant to the enquiry is collected and structured. Details on data handling are set out in our privacy documentation.

What does implementation involve?

Five stages: mapping your current process, building the workflow, connecting your systems, testing, and launch.

Can I start with one workflow?

Yes. Most implementations start with a single, well-defined workflow — such as seller enquiries — before expanding further.
