Why roofing companies

Why roofing leads specifically need an instant response

Roofing leads don't behave like most other home service inquiries — and that's exactly what Structio's roofing marketing automation and AI lead qualification are built around.

Storm surges create sudden urgency

A single storm can generate dozens of leads in the same day, all shopping several contractors at once. Whoever replies first usually wins the inspection.

Insurance-driven timelines

Many roofing jobs run on insurance claim deadlines. Homeowners want a contractor who can move quickly — and a fast first response signals exactly that.

Crews are in the field, not the office

Roofing teams are on roofs, not answering phones. Leads that come in during the workday often wait hours for a reply — long enough to lose the job.
