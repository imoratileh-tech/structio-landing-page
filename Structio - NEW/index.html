01 • Diagnostic Analysis

The operational friction that bleeds real-estate pipeline.

In high-volume real-estate markets, deals are rarely lost to lack of interest. They are lost in the minutes and hours immediately after an enquiry arrives — swallowed by disconnected inboxes, buried WhatsApp threads, and unorganized spreadsheets.

CHALLENGE 01

Incoming Enquiries Being Missed

Portal notifications from Property24, Private Property, and direct website forms flood personal agent inboxes after hours and on weekends. High-intent prospective buyers receive silence when interest is peak.

CHALLENGE 02

Delayed & Irregular Responses

Prospective buyers often contact multiple listings. Long response delays can mean they move on before an agent replies.

CHALLENGE 03

Information Scattered Across Silos

Buyer budget details remain locked in one agent's private WhatsApp chat. Viewing notes stay on paper pads. CRM records are half-filled or neglected entirely, crippling management visibility.

CHALLENGE 04

Poorly Qualified Prospects

Agents can spend time on enquiries from people whose budget, financing, or timing does not match the listing.

CHALLENGE 05

Inconsistent Follow-Up

Without automated cadences, leads that do not convert on the first viewing vanish into cold archives. No systematic check-ins exist to re-engage prospects when matching stock arrives.

CHALLENGE 06

Repetitive Administrative Exhaustion

Skilled negotiators spend their mornings copying names into spreadsheets, typing the same property specifications repeatedly, and manually asking basic qualifying questions.

BILLING POLICY
Transparent Enquiry Counting Rules

We maintain strict commercial honesty. How we define a lead determines exactly what you pay for.

  • One Unique Enquiry = One Lead: Every unique new incoming enquiry counts as exactly one lead, whether or not the prospect completes the qualification conversation.
  • No Message Billing: Additional follow-up messages, conversational turns, brochure deliveries, and AI responses do NOT count as additional leads.
  • Automatic Deduplication: Repeated submissions from the same telephone number or email within a 30-day window are deduplicated and never double-counted.
  • No Automatic Overage Charges: Usage tracking is strictly separated from billing. If your volume surges, your infrastructure continues running seamlessly while we discuss package expansion.
07 • Practical Clarifications

Frequently asked questions.

Direct, technical, and commercial answers regarding Structio's enquiry infrastructure.

Which enquiry channels can Structio connect to? +
Structio can be set up to collect enquiries from WhatsApp Business, portal email notifications, and website forms, then send them to your chosen CRM. Integrations are confirmed during discovery.
How does the AI qualification actually work? Does it make things up? +
Qualification follows your agency's criteria, such as budget, financing, timing, and area. Questions outside approved listing details are flagged for an agent.
How does an agent take over the conversation? +
When a lead needs a person, the agent receives the qualification notes and CRM record, then continues the conversation through the agreed channel.
