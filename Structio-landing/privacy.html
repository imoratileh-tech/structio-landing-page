Privacy notice

This notice explains how Structio handles information submitted through the consultation form on this website.

How your information is used

Structio uses the information you provide to review and respond to your consultation request, contact you about arranging a meeting, and understand the enquiry workflow you describe.

Information collected

The form asks for your name, agency or team name, work email address, phone number, team size, and any meeting preferences or context you choose to share. Do not include passwords, financial account details, or other sensitive personal information.

Form provider

The form is submitted to a serverless function hosted by Vercel and sent to Structio through Resend, an email delivery provider. These providers process the information to provide hosting and email delivery, subject to their own privacy terms: Vercel's privacy policy and Resend's privacy policy.

Retention and requests

Structio retains enquiry information only as long as needed to respond and manage the resulting business conversation, then deletes it when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable record-keeping requirements. Email providers may retain messages under their own retention practices. To ask about a consultation enquiry or request correction or deletion, email info@structio.com.

Updates

This notice may be updated when the form or its data handling changes. Please review it again before submitting a request if you have concerns.
